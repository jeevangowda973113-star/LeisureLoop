Property Damage & Responsibility

Effective date: 11 November 2026
We want every LeisureLoop visit to be enjoyable and respectful. Please use each venue carefully, follow the venue owner's instructions, and treat the property and other visitors with care.

1. Customer responsibility for damage

Customers are responsible for damage to a venue, equipment, furniture, fixtures or other property when the damage is caused by their actions, negligence, misuse, failure to follow reasonable venue rules, or the actions of people in their booking group. This does not make a customer responsible for ordinary wear and tear, pre-existing damage, or damage they did not cause.

2. Reporting and resolving damage

If damage occurs or is noticed during a booking, the customer should inform the venue owner promptly and contact LeisureLoop through the support contact shown on the website. Venue owners should also notify the customer and LeisureLoop promptly and provide reasonable supporting information, such as dated photographs, an incident description and an itemised estimate or invoice where available.

Any discussion about responsibility or compensation should be based on the available facts. Customers should have a reasonable opportunity to respond. LeisureLoop may help pass on information and facilitate communication, but does not determine legal liability or guarantee recovery of any amount.

3. Venue owner responsibilities

Venue owners are responsible for managing and maintaining their premises, equipment and facilities, setting and communicating reasonable safety and usage rules, and ensuring that the space is suitable for the activity offered. Owners should identify known restrictions and pre-existing damage before a booking begins.

4. LeisureLoop's role

LeisureLoop provides an online marketplace to help customers discover venues and send booking requests. Unless expressly stated otherwise for a particular service, LeisureLoop does not own, possess, operate, inspect or control the listed venues and is not the provider of the venue's activities or facilities.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, LeisureLoop is not responsible for loss or damage to a venue's property caused by a customer's or visitor's acts or omissions. This policy does not exclude or limit any responsibility that cannot legally be excluded or limited, including liability arising from LeisureLoop's own acts or omissions where applicable law provides otherwise.

5. No automatic charges or admission of liability

A damage report does not automatically establish that a customer is liable, and LeisureLoop will not treat a report alone as proof. Any payment or reimbursement should be discussed directly between the affected parties and supported by reasonable evidence. Do not submit false, exaggerated or misleading damage claims.

6. Questions

For questions about this policy or to report a booking-related property issue, contact LeisureLoop using the contact details published on our website. Please include the booking details and a clear description of the concern. Do not send sensitive payment credentials or passwords.
