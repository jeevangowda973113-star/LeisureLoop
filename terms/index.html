Venue owner terms

Property Damage & Owner Responsibility

Effective date: 11 November 2026
These terms apply to venue owners who list or make their spaces available through LeisureLoop. By submitting a venue for listing or accepting booking requests through the platform, you acknowledge the responsibilities below.

1. Owner responsibility for the property

You, as the venue owner or authorised operator, remain responsible for the ownership, possession, management, maintenance and condition of your venue, including its premises, equipment, furniture, fixtures and facilities. You are responsible for deciding whether to accept a booking and for setting and communicating reasonable venue rules.

2. Property damage during bookings

LeisureLoop is a marketplace that helps customers discover venues and send booking requests. Unless separately agreed in writing, LeisureLoop does not own, possess, operate, supervise or control your venue and does not take custody of your property. The venue owner is responsible for inspecting and documenting the condition of the property before and after bookings, and for addressing damage at the venue.

Where damage may have been caused by a customer or visitor, you should notify the customer and LeisureLoop promptly and keep reasonable supporting evidence, such as dated photographs, an incident description and an itemised estimate or invoice. Any claim for compensation should be directed to the person believed to have caused the damage and assessed on its facts. A report alone does not establish that a customer is liable.

3. Customer compensation for proven damage

If a customer or a member of their booking group is found, based on reasonable evidence, to have caused damage to the venue or its property through misuse, negligence or intentional conduct, the customer may be required to compensate the venue owner for the reasonable and documented cost of repairing the damage or replacing the affected item where repair is not reasonably possible, subject to applicable law.

The owner should provide supporting evidence, such as dated before-and-after photographs, an incident description and an itemised repair estimate, invoice or replacement-cost record. The customer must be given a reasonable opportunity to review the evidence and respond before a compensation amount is agreed or pursued. Ordinary wear and tear, pre-existing damage and unsupported claims are not chargeable under this clause.

No automatic penalty or fixed multiplier, including a charge of three times the property's value, applies. Compensation must be proportionate to the actual, evidenced loss and must comply with applicable law. LeisureLoop does not automatically determine fault, guarantee recovery or collect compensation unless a separate platform process is expressly introduced and communicated.

4. LeisureLoop is not the property insurer

To the extent permitted by applicable law, LeisureLoop is not responsible for loss of or damage to your venue, equipment or other property caused by customers, visitors, third parties, accidents, ordinary use or events outside LeisureLoop's reasonable control. LeisureLoop does not provide property insurance, guarantee that a customer will pay for damage, or guarantee recovery of compensation from a customer.

You are responsible for assessing your insurance needs and arranging suitable insurance for your premises, equipment, staff and activities. Do not assume that listing your venue on LeisureLoop provides insurance cover.

5. Owner safety and disclosure duties

You must keep the venue reasonably safe and suitable for the activities you advertise, comply with applicable laws and permissions, and clearly disclose material restrictions, hazards, capacity limits and usage rules before a booking is confirmed. You must not list a venue unless you are authorised to do so.

6. No exclusion of non-excludable liability

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable law, including any liability arising from LeisureLoop's own acts or omissions where the law does not permit that liability to be excluded.

7. Owner acknowledgement

By listing your venue or using LeisureLoop's owner features, you confirm that you have read and understood these owner terms, that the information you provide about your venue is accurate, and that you will manage your property and any damage concerns responsibly.

Important: This is a draft platform policy, not legal advice. Before onboarding owners or relying on this wording, have a qualified lawyer in India review it and make sure it matches LeisureLoop's actual services and owner agreement.
